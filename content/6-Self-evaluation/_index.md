During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Company Limited** through the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) Workforce Bootcamp**, scheduled from **July 1 to December 31, 2026**, I have had the opportunity to learn about cloud computing, practice deploying AWS services and apply the knowledge I have gained to a real-world project.

In the **RakiBookery** team project, I was responsible for deploying a book recommendation assistant using Amazon Bedrock, integrated with AWS Lambda, Amazon S3 and a Knowledge Base. I participated in developing the Workshop content, testing system functionality and collaborating with team members during deployment, troubleshooting and report completion. Through these activities, I gradually improved my professional knowledge, teamwork and problem-solving skills.

In terms of work ethic, I have made an effort to complete my assigned tasks, receive feedback and support my team members. However, I still need to improve my time management, professional communication and initiative when handling complex problems.
